## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an environment of grounded reassurance, thoughtful pace, and empathetic clinical rigor without clinical sterility. Designed for individuals navigating psychological vulnerability, acute stress, and first-line mental health support, the interface rejects cold techno-optimism and transactional AI aesthetics in favor of calm editorial wellness.

The style unites editorial clarity with soft ambient utility:
- **Atmosphere:** Organic, meditative, safe, and deliberate. Interfaces breathe through generous negative space and rhythmic hierarchy.
- **Visual Movement:** Soft Organic Editorial. Combines the timeless authority of literature with modern, tactile software primitives.
- **Emotional Intent:** To lower cognitive load, reduce nervous system activation, and foster trust through transparent system boundaries and unhurried interactions.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic sanctuary devoid of abrasive alarms or hyper-saturated digital hues. All functional pairings strictly satisfy WCAG AAA standards for body readability.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary (`#486B5A` / `#3A5748`):** Deep Sage. Used for grounding calls-to-action, active status indicators, and confident typographic anchors.
- **Secondary (`#5B8E88` / Soft Teal Light `#E2ECE9`):** Resilient Sea-Foam. Dictates collaborative elements, conversational highlights, interactive chips, and guided breathing interfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6A48` / Light Ochre `#F6EFE6`):** Grounded Earth & Crisis De-escalation. Replaces jarring neon emergency tones with a high-visibility, calm amber-earth register that communicates urgency without inciting panic.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF9F5` / `#F5F4EE`):** Warm Ivory and Oatmeal. Replaces clinical pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to prevent eye fatigue during twilight or late-night crisis interactions.
- **Neutral Ink (`#232926` / Muted `#404944`):** Soft Dark Charcoal. High-contrast legibility that prevents harsh digital glare.
- **Borders & Rules (`#E5E3D8`):** Soft Sand. Tactile, whisper-quiet structural delineations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy intentionally pairs the editorial warmth of **Newsreader** with the functional clarity and open geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Newsreader** handles conversational openers, reflective quotes, reflective wellness questions, and narrative guidance. Its optical sizing conveys human care, literary grace, and clinical poise.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** structures dialogue bubbles, interactive inputs, safety protocols, and real-time triage responses. Its rounded shapes and generous apertures keep text legible across emotional distress and varied visual capabilities.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes a structured, comfortable layout model to preserve focus and eliminate chaotic screen noise:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Centered 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1180px` for general portals, and restricted to `760px` for high-intimacy conversational flows. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem` (`24px`), margins at `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column responsive grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with dynamic safe area offsets, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding.

Horizontal layouts never compress interaction targets. Vertical rhythms lean on unhurried gaps (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between conversational threads and response suggestions to create natural cognitive breathers.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on muted surface differentiation and warm ambient light rather than artificial drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Floor:* Primary canvas `#FAF9F5`.
  - *Raised Layer:* Cards and modal surfaces `#F5F4EE` or `#FFFFFF`.
  - *Subtle Nested Canvas:* Chat reply anchors and text areas `#F0EFE8`.
- **Ambient Shadows:** Surfaces lift with diffuse, low-contrast shadows tinted with dark forest charcoal:
  - *Resting Elevation:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(35, 41, 38, 0.04)`.
  - *Floating / Active Elevation:* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(35, 41, 38, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(35, 41, 38, 0.03)`.
- **Structural Outlines:** Every card and modal contains a solid `1px` low-contrast stroke of `#E5E3D8`, anchoring components against changing display backlights without sharp contrast jumps.

## Shapes

Shapes across the design system prioritize soft geometry:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Styled with `rounded-2xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to create smooth, approachable envelopes.
- **Action Buttons & Form Fields:** Standardized at `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) for structured tactility.
- **Interactive Suggestion Pills & Dialogue Badges:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provides a distinctly clickable, human, and lightweight touchpoint.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#486B5A` fill, `#FAF9F5` typography, `1rem` border-radius. Active/Hover shifts smoothly to `#3A5748`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Soft sand background (`#E2ECE9`) with `#3A5748` text, transitioning on hover to a `1px` inner border of `#5B8E88`.
- **Sizing:** Minimum touch target height of `48px` to guarantee physical accessibility during motor tension or hand tremors.

### Chat Stream & Response Bubbles
- **System / AI Voice:** Warm ivory container (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` solid `#E5E3D8` stroke. Left-aligned with Newsreader titles and Plus Jakarta Sans conversational prose.
- **User Voice:** Grounded `#486B5A` surface with crisp off-white text, or warm tinted sage container (`#E2ECE9`) with dark charcoal (`#232926`) text.
- **Streaming State:** Gentle pulsing organic sage dot indicator without aggressive mechanical typing flickers.

### Chips & Quick Responses
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) interactive chips resting on `#F5F4EE` with `1px` border of `#E5E3D8`. Text set in `label-md`. 
- Provides one-tap emotional state expressions (e.g., "I feel overwhelmed," "Guide my breathing," "Just listen").

### Cards
- Standard modules leverage a `24px` radius, `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F4EE` surface, and soft border (`#E5E3D8`).
- Inner card padding strictly adopts `space-lg` (`24px`).

### Crisis Safety Card
- Dedicated priority component triggered by acute distress detection.
- **Color Treatment:** Grounded warm ochre and terracotta border (`#8C6A48`) over a soothing `#F6EFE6` background. Avoids alarming pure reds.
- **Content Structure:** Immediate one-tap direct access to human crisis lines (phone, SMS), concise supportive text, and a non-dismissible, steady interface anchor.

### Inputs & Text Areas
- Uncluttered fields with `#FAF9F5` backgrounds, inset padding of `16px`, and an active focus state defined by a `1.5px` border of `#486B5A` alongside a soft ambient glow. Placeholders are cast in comforting, non-demanding language.
