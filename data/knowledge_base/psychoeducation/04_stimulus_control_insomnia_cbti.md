## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
The gold-standard protocol to extinguish negative conditioned arousal between your bed and waking anxiety. Includes the '20-minute bed detachment' rule.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
1. Use bed only for sleep (no screens, work, or active reading).
2. 20-Minute Rule: If awake after ~20 minutes, leave bedroom calmly.
3. Dim lighting: Sit quietly until drowsy; return to bed only when sleepy.
4. Fixed wake time: Wake at identical time daily regardless of sleep duration.

---

## 4. Clinical Limitations & Boundaries
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Behavioral sleep protocol. Chronic insomnia exceeding 3 months requires formal evaluation by a sleep physician.
