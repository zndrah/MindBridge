## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
Provides structured, non-clinical educational strategies for students experiencing academic pressure, test anxiety, or study-related overwhelm. Focuses on practical study planning, manageable study breaks, calibrating realistic expectations, and seeking appropriate human support.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
1. Practical Study Planning: Break study material into small, manageable focus blocks (such as 25 to 45 minutes) followed by scheduled review, rather than marathon cramming sessions.
2. Manageable Study Breaks: Take restorative 5- to 15-minute breaks completely away from study desks and screens to allow cognitive consolidation and nervous system recovery.
3. Realistic Expectations: Focus on steady effort and achievable daily milestones rather than perfection, reminding yourself that personal worth is not defined by test scores or academic grades.
4. Appropriate Human-Support Guidance: Reach out to academic advisors, study groups, trusted mentors, friends, or campus counseling resources when feeling overwhelmed or falling behind.

## 4. Clinical Limitations & Boundaries
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Non-clinical educational guidance for study-related stress. For persistent or severe mental distress, seek support from school counseling services or a qualified healthcare professional.
