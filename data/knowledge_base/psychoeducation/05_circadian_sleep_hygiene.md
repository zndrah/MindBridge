## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
Practical evening protocols adapted for Indian climatic rhythms and light environments, including blue-light cutoffs and dinner timing guidelines.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
1. Dinner Timing: Finish dinner at least 2.5 hours prior to sleep.
2. Light Management: Dim overhead fluorescent/LED lights 1 hour before sleep.
3. Ventilation: Ensure comfortable cross-ventilation or fan temperature in bedroom.
4. Morning Sun: 10 minutes of natural outdoor sunlight within 1 hour of waking.
