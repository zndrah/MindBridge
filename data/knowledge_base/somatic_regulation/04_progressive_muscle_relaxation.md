## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
Jacobson's clinical sequence targeting 14 to 16 muscle groups. Trains proprioceptive awareness to distinguish involuntary neuromuscular stress tension from intentional ease.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
Sequentially isolate muscle groups (feet, calves, thighs, hands, arms, shoulders, face):
1. Inhale and gently tighten muscle group for 5-7 seconds (avoid painful cramping).
2. Exhale and suddenly release tension completely.
3. Notice the warm, tingling contrast for 15-20 seconds before moving to next group.

---

## 4. Clinical Limitations & Boundaries
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do not tense areas with recent injuries, muscle strains, or chronic pain conditions.
