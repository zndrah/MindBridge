## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
Step-by-step external sensory scanning to re-anchor the nervous system to the immediate environment during sensory overload or dissociative anxiety.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
Scan your current room and identify:
- 5 items you can visibly see
- 4 physical surfaces you can touch
- 3 sounds in the quiet background
- 2 distinct scents in ambient air
- 1 taste or physical sensation in mouth

---

## 4. Clinical Limitations & Boundaries
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Supportive grounding tool. In cases of traumatic flashbacks, modify sensory cues to prioritize physical comfort.
