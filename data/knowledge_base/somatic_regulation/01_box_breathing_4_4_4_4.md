## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
A real-time 4-4-4-4 physiological reset. Regulates blood carbon dioxide levels, calming the amygdala during acute emotional surges.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
1. Inhale gently through the nose for 4 seconds.
2. Hold the breath calmly for 4 seconds.
3. Exhale slowly through the mouth for 4 seconds.
4. Rest in stillness for 4 seconds.
Repeat for 2 to 4 cycles.

---

## 4. Clinical Limitations & Boundaries
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Box breathing is a non-clinical supportive exercise for acute stress de-escalation. It is not a treatment for panic disorders or respiratory conditions.
