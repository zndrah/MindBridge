## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
Learn the clinical 'Thought Court' framework: dissect intrusive catastrophic worst-case thoughts by weighing factual empirical evidence versus automatic fear bias.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
1. State the intrusive thought as a factual claim.
2. Identify the distortion: Catastrophizing, All-or-Nothing, or Fortune-Telling.
3. Prosecuting evidence: What concrete proof confirms this worst-case outcome?
4. Defense evidence: What facts prove I have survived or coped with this before?
5. Balanced Verdict: Formulate an evidence-grounded alternative statement.
