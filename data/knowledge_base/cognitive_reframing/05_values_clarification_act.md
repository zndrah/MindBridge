## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT) matrix for individuals experiencing burnout numbness. Identifies authentic personal compass points across career, relationships, and health.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
1. Identify three life domains: Health, Meaningful Work, Social Connection.
2. Write down one core personal value for each (e.g., 'integrity', 'curiosity', 'compassion').
3. Select one small, concrete action aligned with that value to complete today regardless of emotional weather.
