## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
Systematic micro-dosing of rewarding tasks designed to reverse negative dopaminergic feedback loops without overwhelming cognitive load.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
1. Identify one micro-task requiring less than 3 minutes (e.g., drink a glass of water, open window blinds).
2. Disconnect action from motivation: do not wait to 'feel like doing it'.
3. Record completion and rate emotional state (0-10) before and after.

---

## 4. Clinical Limitations & Boundaries
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Supportive behavioral pacing module. Severe clinical depression with vegetative symptoms requires professional medical care.
