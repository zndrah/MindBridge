## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
Techniques to identify Catastrophizing and Fortune-Telling distortions. Includes an evidence-backed 'Worry Postponement' routine to disengage nocturnal rumination.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
1. When intrusive worries arise at night, acknowledge them without arguing.
2. Write them onto a paper log labeled 'Actionable Tomorrow'.
3. Schedule a 15-minute dedicated 'Worry Window' the following afternoon (e.g., 4:00 PM).
4. Return focus to somatic bed anchors; remind yourself that nocturnal rumination produces false urgency.
