## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
A 9-item standardized self-report inventory evaluating the frequency and severity of depressive symptoms over the preceding two weeks, including Item 9 assessing thoughts of self-harm or that one would be better off dead.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
Scores range from 0 to 27:
- **0–4:** Minimal or absent depressive symptoms
- **5–9:** Mild depressive symptoms
- **10–14:** Moderate depressive symptoms
- **15–19:** Moderately severe depressive symptoms
- **20–27:** Severe depressive symptoms

---

## 4. Clinical Limitations & Mandatory Safety Protocols
> [!CRITICAL]
> ### 1. Non-Diagnostic Instrument
> The PHQ-9 is an informational psychometric screening rubric, **NOT a diagnostic confirmation**. MindBridge cannot and will not diagnose major depressive disorder or any psychiatric illness based on PHQ-9 scores. Elevated scores warrant evaluation by a licensed healthcare professional.

> [!WARNING]
> ### 2. Item 9 Safety Stratification Protocol
> - **Never a Standalone Suicide Assessment:** The PHQ-9 must **never** be used as a standalone suicide-risk assessment instrument.
> - **Immediate Safety Follow-up:** Any positive endorsement of Item 9 (*"Thoughts that you would be better off dead, or of hurting yourself in some way"*) requires immediate, direct safety follow-up.
> - **Do Not Auto-Equate with Imminent Suicide:** A positive Item 9 endorsement must **not** be automatically equated with imminent suicide risk. The system must stratify risk across:
>   1. *Passive death wishes* (e.g. desire for escape or relief from exhaustion without intent).
>   2. *Nonsuicidal self-injury / self-harm thoughts*.
>   3. *Active suicidal ideation*.
>   4. *Explicit intent*.
>   5. *Specific plan*.
>   6. *Access to lethal means*.
>   7. *Imminent timeframe*.
> - **Deterministic Safety Routing:**
>   - *Imminent Peril Indicated (Intent + Plan + Means + Immediate Timeframe):* Immediately bypass ordinary RAG generation and deterministically present emergency crisis guidance (Tele-MANAS, 988, 112).
>   - *Ambiguous or Passive Risk:* Prioritize compassionate, supportive follow-up, offer confidential human crisis assistance (e.g. Tele-MANAS, Crisis Text Line), and avoid punitive escalation or unsolicited police dispatch.
