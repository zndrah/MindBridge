## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
Appraises the degree to which life situations over the past month are appraised as unpredictable, uncontrollable, and overloading.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
Scores (0 to 40):
- 0-13: Low stress
- 14-26: Moderate stress
- 27-40: High perceived stress

---

## 4. Clinical Limitations & Boundaries
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Psychometric screening tool measuring perceived subjective stress. It does not measure clinical pathology or mental illness.
