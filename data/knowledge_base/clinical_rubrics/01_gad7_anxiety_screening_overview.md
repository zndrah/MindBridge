## 2. Summary & Operational Objective
7-item standardized self-report inventory evaluating frequency of anxiety symptoms over the past 2 weeks (nervousness, uncontrollable worry, restlessness, irritability).

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
Scores (0 to 21):
- 0-4: Minimal anxiety
- 5-9: Mild anxiety
- 10-14: Moderate anxiety
- 15-21: Severe anxiety

---

## 4. Clinical Limitations & Boundaries
> [!IMPORTANT]
> CRITICAL: The GAD-7 is a self-report screening rubric, NOT a diagnostic instrument. MindBridge cannot and will not diagnose generalized anxiety disorder based on this scale. Elevated scores warrant evaluation by a licensed healthcare professional.
