## 2. Summary & Operational Objective

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions & Protocol Details
Clinical Red Lines:
1. Active Suicidal Ideation: Immediate hard-bypass to Tele-MANAS 14416 / 112.
2. Psychotic / Hallucinatory States: Immediate referral to nearest tertiary psychiatric center.
3. Medication Inquiries: Strict refusal; refer to licensed prescriber.
4. Low-Acuity Distress: Safe zone for somatic grounding and psychoeducation.
